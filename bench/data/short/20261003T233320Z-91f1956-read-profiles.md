# Official streaming-read configuration: short paired experiment

Only async clients are measured. Full/Stream describe uploads; all responses are streamed to EOF.

This is our Rust TLS echo benchmark with selected upstream streaming-read settings, not the original upstream benchmark. The upstream benchmark fully consumes small responses, which is deliberately not changed here.

Natural and official variants are adjacent and alternate order across rounds. Three short rounds are not confidence intervals or a stable universal ranking. CPU and IO can be shared with other programs.
